I've always wanted to build this kind of thing: a website for my own soul to come out and be seen by others.
I'm surely afraid of what this could bring me to (I don't know, I don't like negative comments lol, so please be kind).
But, in a way, this is the only way that I have to organize the thoughts in my head, write a bit about me in a more thoughtful way and try to improve myself over time.

The people that do this and I've been taking inspiration from are several. I have to cite the one I like the most: _Derek Sivers_. 
I don't know how to label him. I just know he's a nice, kind and clever human being :)
I'd like to become like him one day but the road is very long and I'm getting older and older :(
Go check out his blog at [sive.rs]

Basically AI built the whole website, gave me advices on what to do, gave me options, but the guy that decides in the end is always me. I'm like a Project Manager telling his slaves what to do and how I want things and waiting dor them to do the work.
I don't even know why I'm pretending to know what a Project Manager is.
I've never worked in a serious company.
Hope you enjoy this thing.
You should do it too!
Each one of us has something to say.
