# 0039 - Numeric dates on Italian pages read day/month/year

Status: Accepted · 2026-11-22 · Owner request · Amends [0016](0016-frontend-pages.md) (pages) and
[0023](0023-simplification-pass.md) (the entry's details block)

**Context.** Visible dates were "2026-11-20" in both languages, and written out ("21 October 2026", "21 ottobre 2026")
in the details block of an entry. An Italian reader writes 20/11/2026.

**The rule (owner, 2026-11-23).** An Italian date that used to be shown as numbers (2026-10-21) is now 21/10/2026. A date
that was already written out in words stays written out. English pages change nothing.

## Decisions

1. **One helper**, `displayDate(locale, date, words?)` in `src/lib/dates.ts` (no imports, so the build, node:test and the
   Worker all use it). Shown as numbers: English "2026-11-20", Italian "20/11/2026". With `words`: English "20 November
   2026", Italian "20 novembre 2026". Everything is read in UTC.
2. **Where each form is used.** As numbers (Italian now day/month/year): the journal table (home, journal index), the
   "Aggiornato il" line on Now, the date under a photo, and the date of a comment (the Worker, per page language). In
   words (unchanged in both languages): the details block of an entry; that was the only written-out date any helper
   touched, so no other place needed restoring. `longDate` was folded into `displayDate(..., true)`.
3. **Machine-readable dates do not change.** `isoDate` (moved to the same file) still feeds the `datetime` attribute of
   `<time>`, the sitemap, feeds, structured data, the Worker's manifest and file names. No template writes a date
   without going through one of the two functions.
4. The Privacy page's "Last updated: [launch date]" / its Italian line is owner text with a placeholder, not a formatted
   date, and is not touched.

## Consequences

- `tests/unit/dates.test.ts` (both languages, padding, UTC), `tests/e2e/dates.spec.ts` (every page type, both languages,
  `datetime` ISO, sitemap, feed, JSON-LD) and `tests/worker/comments.test.ts` (comment dates).
- `entry` baselines (win32) did not change for this decision (the details block is as before); the `home-it` ones were
  regenerated by a full `pnpm test:visual --update-snapshots` (single-page regeneration gave baselines that failed in the
  full run) and the Italian dates in them were looked at.
